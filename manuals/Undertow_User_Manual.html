Meet Undertow

Meet Undertow

Undertow produces three simultaneous signals from a shared oscillator: SINE, MORPH, and a square-wave SUB one octave below. Use SINE for a rounded fundamental, MORPH for evolving harmonics, and SUB for bass reinforcement. All three outputs support up to 16 voices.

Morph grows out of a sine. At zero, MORPH starts as a sine-like waveform. Turning it up raises and widens a triangular fold from the sine trough. Edge hardness changes the transition into that fold; the Asymmetry menu adds a small lift to one side.

First patch

First patch

  1. Connect SINE to a VCA, then to your mixer. Open the VCA or patch an envelope to its level input.
  2. Patch your sequencer or keyboard pitch to V/Oct. The default base frequency is about 261.63 Hz (C4); adjust Frequency and Fine tune as needed.
  3. Listen to MORPH instead and sweep the Morph knob with its CV input empty.
  4. Mix in SUB for a square wave one octave below. Leave S-Gate empty for continuous sub output.
  5. For animated timbre, patch a positive envelope or offset LFO to Morph CV and turn Morph up to set the maximum depth.

Panel tour

Panel tour

Click a numbered marker to reach its description. Purple jacks are inputs; cyan jacks are outputs. All three outputs run simultaneously.

1

FREQ

Upper left · 10 Hz–10 kHz

Sets the base oscillator frequency with a logarithmic response. The default is about 261.63 Hz (C4). Use OCT for octave steps, or leave it off for continuous tuning.

2

FINE

Below FREQ · −100 to +100 cents

Offsets the base pitch by up to one semitone in either direction. Center is zero. Fine tuning stays active when OCT is enabled.

3

OCT

Gold button below FINE

Switches FREQ between continuous tuning and octave steps referenced to C4. The button lights in stepped mode. V/OCT and EXPO still apply continuous pitch offsets.

4

LIN FM

Lower left knob · 0–100%

Sets modulation depth for the jack directly above it. Start at zero and increase gradually. The upper 20% adds nonlinear drive to the modulation bus; the input is AC-coupled, so use EXPO for sustained pitch offsets.

5

EDGE

Small lower center knob · 0–100%

Controls the hardness of the morph fold boundary. Turn down for a softer transition or up for a sharper edge. Default: 50%.

6

MORPH

Lower right knob · 0–100%

Raises and widens a triangular fold from the sine trough. With the jack above it empty, the knob directly sets the morph amount. With CV patched, it sets depth: knob × voltage / 8 V, clamped to 0–100%.

7

Waveform preview

Display below the knobs

Shows the morph waveform and a frequency readout. Watch the fold develop as you turn MORPH and EDGE. The Preview tracer context-menu option toggles the tracer.

8

V/OCT — pitch input

Upper right jack

Tracks pitch at one octave per volt, added to FREQ and FINE. Patch a keyboard or sequencer here. Polyphonic pitch supports up to 16 voices.

9

SYNC — input & light

Left jack below FREQ

A rising trigger resets the main oscillator phase for hard-sync sounds. The small light beside the jack indicates sync activity. Patch another oscillator here and sweep FREQ to explore changing harmonics.

10

S GATE — input & light

Right jack above MORPH

Gates SUB only. With no cable, SUB runs continuously. With a cable, voltages below 1 V mute SUB; 1 V or higher enables it. A rising gate resets the sub-divider state. The nearby light indicates a patched high gate.

11

EXPO — pitch input

Center jack above EDGE

Adds exponential frequency modulation at one octave per volt, summed with V/OCT. Use an external attenuator for small vibrato or pitch-envelope amounts. Unlike LIN FM, this input passes sustained pitch offsets.

12

LIN FM — modulation input

Left jack above the LIN FM knob

Feeds the linear FM bus. The knob below sets depth. DC and very slow changes are rejected by AC coupling around 4.9 Hz. The oscillator stays within 8 Hz–20 kHz rather than crossing through zero frequency.

13

MORPH — CV input

Right jack above the MORPH knob

Multiplies the MORPH knob setting by voltage / 8 V. At full knob, 0–8 V covers the full morph range; negative values clamp to zero. A cable at 0 V returns the waveform to zero morph, even with the knob raised.

14

SINE — output

Bottom left cyan jack

Approximately ±5 V sine-like signal at the main oscillator pitch. Analog character adds subtle soft saturation by default; disable it in the context menu for the cleaner underlying wave.

15

SUB — output

Bottom center cyan jack

Square wave one octave below the main oscillator, limited to ±5 V. Use S GATE to switch this layer rhythmically while SINE and MORPH continue. There is one sub output.

16

MORPH — output

Bottom right cyan jack

Folded waveform at the main oscillator pitch, limited to ±5 V. Its timbre follows MORPH, EDGE, Morph CV, and the Asymmetry setting. This output is simultaneous with SINE and SUB.

Inputs and outputs

Inputs and outputs

Control / connectionBehavior
V/Oct inputPitch tracking at one octave per volt. Added to coarse and fine tuning.
Expo FM inputAn additional exponential pitch input at one octave per volt, summed with V/Oct. Use an external attenuator for subtle modulation.
Linear FM inputAC-coupled frequency modulation, with DC rejection around 4.9 Hz. The amount knob controls depth. A normalized 10 V signal corresponds to a base-frequency-sized deviation before drive. Frequency stays positive and is limited to 8 Hz–20 kHz.
Morph CV inputEffective morph = clamp(knob × voltage / 8 V, 0, 1). At full knob, 0–8 V spans the full morph range. Negative voltages clamp to zero. Inserting a cable replaces the knob-only setting with this multiplication.
Sync inputA rising trigger resets the main oscillator phase for hard-sync effects. The adjacent light indicates sync activity.
S-Gate inputControls SUB only. Unpatched: continuous sub. Patched: below 1 V mutes it; at or above 1 V enables it. A rising gate resets the sub divider state. The light indicates a patched high gate.
SINE outputApproximately ±5 V sine-like output. Analog character is enabled by default.
MORPH outputFolded waveform, limited to ±5 V. Shares the main oscillator pitch with SINE.
SUB outputOne square-wave output at half the main oscillator frequency (−1 octave), limited to ±5 V. There is no separate −2 octave output.

Voice count follows the largest channel count on any input, up to 16. All three outputs use that voice count. A monophonic control cable can address all voices through Rack’s normal polyphonic input behavior.

Patch recipes

Patch recipes

Moving fold

Send MORPH through a VCA. Patch a 0–8 V envelope to Morph CV and raise Morph to taste. Use edge hardness to choose a smoother or sharper fold.

FM voice

Patch another oscillator’s sine to Linear FM. Start with the amount at zero, then raise it gradually. Explore the upper fifth of the knob for a more driven modulation response.

Gated bass layer

Mix SINE and SUB. Send a rhythmic gate to S-Gate so the sub enters only on selected notes while the main oscillator continues. Use a separate VCA envelope to shape the complete voice.

Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting

Morph CV has no effect

Raise the Morph knob. With CV patched, the knob multiplies the incoming voltage; zero knob means zero morph. Use positive CV for the full range.

The sub is silent

Remove the S-Gate cable or send a gate at least 1 V high. S-Gate does not gate SINE or MORPH.

A slow signal does not bend Linear FM as expected

Linear FM rejects DC and very slow changes. Use Expo FM or V/Oct for sustained pitch offsets and slow pitch modulation.

Octave steps do not quantize my sequence

Stepping applies only to the coarse Frequency knob. Fine tune and the pitch inputs still add continuous offsets.
