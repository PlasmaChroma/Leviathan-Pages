Connect the deck

Connect the deck

  1. Add Temporal Deck and feed it audio, or load a sample.
  2. Use the deck’s small TD.Scope button to spawn its companion, or add TD.Scope from the module browser.
  3. Place TD.Scope immediately to the right of Temporal Deck, touching it in the same row. The deck must be its left neighbor.
  4. Play or record some audio. The display receives waveform and playback-position data through the expander connection; no patch cables are needed.
Pair it with Temporal Deck. TD.Scope has no audio or CV jacks and cannot act as a standalone oscilloscope for other modules.

Read and drag the waveform

Read and drag the waveform

The vertical waveform view shows the deck’s audio history or sample material. Time runs along the vertical axis and signal amplitude extends horizontally. A yellow horizontal line marks the playback head. Stereo view places the channels side by side.

Click and hold within the display to hold playback position, then drag vertically to scrub through the material. Keeping the mouse still while holding maintains the hold; release to end the gesture. This controls the deck and can change what you hear. SLIP and the other deck transport settings govern the resulting playback behavior.

Inverted Vertical reverses the display’s time orientation and adjusts drag direction with it.

Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting

The display is not connected

Move TD.Scope directly against the right edge of Temporal Deck in the same row. Another module or a gap between them prevents the expander link.

Connected, but no useful waveform

Make sure the deck contains recorded audio or a loaded sample. Check the deck’s input and playback state. Try Auto (window peak) and increase Brightness if the trace is hard to see.

Dragging changes the sound

The display is also a playback control. Dragging scrubs the deck’s playback position; release the mouse to finish. Consult the deck manual for SLIP, FREEZE, and playback modes.
